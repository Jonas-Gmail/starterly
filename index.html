Restore a purchase

On a new iPhone, open Starterly, go to Settings and tap "Restore purchases". You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

My starter peaks at a different time than predicted

Log the peak with "It peaked" after each feeding and note the room temperature. After a few feedings Starterly adjusts its predictions to your starter.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Starterly ("the app") is developed by Jonas Volk. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

No data collection

Starterly does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (starters, feedings, temperatures, notes and bake plans) is stored only on your device. If you use iCloud device backups, this data may be included in your backup under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information. Apple shares only anonymous, aggregated sales information with developers.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
